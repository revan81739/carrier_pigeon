# Carrier Pigeon

A short 2D sidescroller based in Middle Earth where you are a pigeon racing to deliver the last message of a king under siege to a mysterious hermit atop a mountain.

## Controls & Gameplay

Controls: WASD

Goal: Reach the other side of the map beyond the forest, climb the mountain, and deliver the message to the hermit on top.

After avoiding the catapult and archers, you reach the checkpoint (the flag right before the forest). The forest is riddled with deadly booby traps, archer towers, and a mage tower at the end of the forest. The mystical floating rune in the forest is... well, go find out.
(note:to move past the dialogue, pressing space **might** work, otherwise clicking the arrow on the bottom right should do the trick. yeah i know)

## Development

The game was made in Godot using GDScript, with LibreSprite for the art (most of it—credit for anything not made by me is listed below).

I made this project largely for learning Godot. Learning GDScript wasn't as hard as I thought (the [syntax reference docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/gdscript/gdscript_basics.html) helped a lot), and issues were mostly related to nodes and other engine-specific mechanics. I'd been planning to learn the basics of Godot for some time, and then Terra YSWS came along, so I thought hey why not.
